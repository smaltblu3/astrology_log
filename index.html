The placements

Traditional significations of each planet involved this season.

Mercury ℞ in Scorpio

Communication, technology, transport, contracts and data. Scorpio adds secrecy, investigation and hidden information.

Venus ℞ (Scorpio → Libra)

Money, payments, value, relationships and agreements. Retrograde from Oct 3; moves back into Libra on Oct 25.

Mars in Leo

Action, force, heat and conflict. Squares Mercury three times across the cycle.

Pluto in Aquarius

Power, control, collective infrastructure and upheaval. Opposed by Mars and squared by Venus this season.

☌

Conjunction (0°): two planets combine and intensify each other.

□

Square (90°): tension that forces action or change.

☍

Opposition (180°): polarity between two forces, often externalised as conflict.

⊤

T-square: an opposition with a third planet square to both; the third planet is the focal point.

☉

Cazimi: a planet within about 17′ of the Sun, the midpoint of its retrograde.
